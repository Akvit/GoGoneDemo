# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Irregular Verbs** mobile application ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

The App is developed by **Pavel Azhel**.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical and usage information.

### Firebase Analytics

The App uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

* app interactions and events;
* screen views and session information;
* information about the device and app;
* identifiers associated with the app or device;
* general diagnostic and usage information.

### Firebase Crashlytics

The App uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to detect, analyze, and troubleshoot crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may collect information such as:

* crash reports and stack traces;
* information about the device and operating system;
* app version and configuration information;
* information about the app's performance around a crash;
* identifiers used to associate crash reports with a particular installation or device.

The information collected by these services is used for analytics, diagnostics, maintaining the App, and improving its reliability.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

The App stores information locally on your device to provide its functionality.

This may include:

* learning progress;
* answers and answer history;
* selected learning settings;
* application preferences.

This information is used to provide the learning functionality of the App and is not used to identify you personally.

## 3. No User Accounts

The App does not provide user accounts or authentication.

You can use the App without registering or logging in.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may be used to:

* understand how the App is used;
* identify and fix technical problems;
* monitor and improve App stability;
* improve the App's functionality and user experience;
* analyze general usage trends.

We do not use the collected information to sell products or services to you.

## 5. Data Sharing

The App uses Google Firebase services provided by Google LLC.

Information collected by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may be transmitted to and processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable policies and terms.

The App does not sell your personal information.

For more information about how Google processes information, please refer to Google's privacy documentation.

## 6. Data Security

Information transmitted to Firebase services is sent using encrypted network connections.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App and its third-party services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention

Data collected by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may be retained by the respective services for the periods defined by their applicable data retention policies.

Locally stored learning data remains on your device until it is deleted by you or the App is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

## 8. Your Choices

The App's core learning functionality does not require Analytics or Crashlytics data collection.

Where applicable, you may control certain analytics or diagnostic data collection through the App's available settings, device settings, or other controls provided by Google and Firebase.

You can also stop the App from accessing or processing locally stored information by uninstalling the App. Data already processed by third-party services may be subject to those services' own retention and deletion policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

## 10. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

* Google Firebase Analytics
* Google Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect and process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
